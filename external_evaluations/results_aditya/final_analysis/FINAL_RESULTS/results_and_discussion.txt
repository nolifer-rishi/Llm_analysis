6. DISCUSSION
==============

The results demonstrate that model behavior depends on the evaluation metric. ROUGE-L, BERTScore-F1, and LLM-based judging capture different characteristics of generated answers and therefore should be interpreted together rather than treated as interchangeable measures.

The unequal number of evaluated questions is an important consideration in interpreting the results. The qwen2.5:1.5b and gemma2:2b models were evaluated on 750 questions each, whereas openai/gpt-oss-120b was evaluated on 350 questions. Consequently, descriptive averages should be interpreted together with the reported sample sizes.

For statistical comparisons, the analysis used only question IDs shared by the two models in each pairwise comparison. This avoids treating unmatched observations as paired observations.

The error analysis provides an additional perspective by examining the types of failures present in low-scoring responses. These categories help distinguish factual errors from incomplete answers, question misunderstanding, irrelevance, unsupported information, and other observed failure modes.

The combined evidence therefore provides a multi-dimensional characterization of model performance rather than relying on a single evaluation metric.
